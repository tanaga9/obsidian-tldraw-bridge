# Arrow selection guidelines

The canonical [Arrow selection guidelines](../integrations/codex/skills/obsidian-tldraw/references/diagramming/arrows.md)
are maintained in the Skill source and included in both distribution builds.
Read that guide for routing, visual connection direction, semantic bindings,
terminal symbols, relationship direction, and provisional binding patterns.
The sections before **Binding patterns (provisional)** are established project
conventions; binding patterns remain under evaluation.

## Evidence and maintenance

The [Arrow binding pattern study](studies/arrow-binding/README.md) preserves the
editable comparison diagram, inspected previews, construction and verification
scripts, environment, and saved-state evidence. These artifacts remain
repository-only; the distributed guide is self-contained.

Edit selection rules and recipes in the canonical guide rather than maintaining
a second copy here. Keep observations and extended rationale in the study.
When evidence changes a recommendation, revise the guide's scope or recipe and
rebuild both distributions following the [development guide](development.md#maintaining-diagramming-guidance).

Prioritize further evaluation of rotated or nonrectangular nodes, open paths,
crowded layouts, parallel arrows, and UI-generated bindings. Record the intent,
complete settings, inspected renderings, behavior after relevant edits, and
saved-state evidence. Distinguish observed behavior from visual preferences and
inferred rules, and recheck after relevant SDK updates.
